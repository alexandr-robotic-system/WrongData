WRONG DATA is an autonomous robotics company based in Chișinău, Moldova. We develop practical hardware and software that connects aerial robots with intelligent ground infrastructure — the drone fleet, the Modular Drone Port and the cloud platform that ties them together.

What we build on.

Open, proven components where they exist; our own hardware and software where they don't.

  • FLIGHT CONTROLArduPilot on STM32H743-class flight controllers; Mission Planner for configuration and missions.
  • NAVIGATIONu-blox ZED-F9P RTK base and rover, GNSS + compass modules; optical flow, VIO and rangefinders under evaluation for GNSS-denied landing.
  • ON-BOARD COMPUTERaspberry Pi 5 as the lead-drone companion computer; ESP32-class bridges for MAVLink and the correction link.
  • AIRFRAMES7-inch and 10-inch quadcopter frames built and configured in-house, one shared parameter baseline across the fleet.
  • COMMAND & CLOUDMAVLink end to end; Telegram, MQTT and AWS IoT Core for remote command, status and the mission library.
  • GROUND STATIONModular Drone Port: AprilTag precision landing, self-centring pads, autonomous charging, weather protection.

Roadmap.

  1. Reliable single-drone loop — launch, mission, return and precision landing on a pad, repeated without a pilot.
  2. Stable long-range link — command, telemetry and RTK corrections proven at site-scale distances, with safe behaviour on link loss.
  3. First Drone Port module — one pad, automatic roof and charging, closed-loop with the drone.
  4. Group of three — lead and followers on one mission, patrol and escort rules in flight.
  5. First pilot site — scheduled perimeter patrols for a real customer, with reports they can act on.
