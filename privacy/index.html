PRIVACY

Privacy notice.

What this website collects, what we do with what you send us, and how footage from a deployed system is handled. Last updated 19 October 2026.

This website

wrongdata.md is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log IP addresses for security purposes under its own privacy terms. The site is served over HTTPS.

When you write to us

If you email alexandr@wrongdata.md or contact us on LinkedIn, we keep your message and contact details for as long as the conversation and any resulting agreement require, and use them only to answer you. We do not add you to mailing lists and do not share your details with third parties.

Footage and data from a deployed system

Video, mission logs and telemetry recorded by a WRONG DATA system on a client's site belong to the client. Retention periods, access rights and deletion are set in the service agreement for that site. Patrol routes are planned to cover the client's own property; public roads and neighbouring property are excluded from routes and camera fields of view wherever physically possible. WRONG DATA staff access recorded data only for support and with the client's consent.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its use, by writing to alexandr@wrongdata.md. We answer within two working days.
