01 / SOLUTIONS

Built for how a site actually needs a response.

Four kinds of sites, one autonomous system underneath. What changes is how it's triggered and what it looks for — not the infrastructure behind it.

01

01 — SECURITY

Private & commercial sites

Homes, gated communities, warehouses, logistics sites, production facilities, parking areas and guarded perimeters.

Responds to intrusion, an alarm signal from an existing security system, suspicious movement, an unfamiliar person, an unknown vehicle or plate, gate/fence/warehouse events, and scheduled perimeter patrols. Instead of sending a person in blind, the system sends drones first, streams video and helps establish what's happening.

02 — AGRICULTURE

Vineyards & farmland

Vineyards, farms, pastures and large plots of land.

Automatic morning or evening flyovers, crop and field condition checks, counting livestock entering pasture, estimating people and equipment on site, farm-perimeter monitoring, and visual checks of set zones after an event or on schedule.

03 — INDUSTRIAL & INFRASTRUCTURE

Large operational sites

Construction sites, solar plants, industrial zones and infrastructure facilities that need regular, safe monitoring of a large area.

Scheduled visual monitoring without a constant manual site visit by an operator.

04 — SMART PROPERTY

Everyday home control

The drone becomes part of how the property is already managed — by voice or scenario: "Show me the perimeter." "Send a drone to the gate." "Run the morning flyover." "Check the vineyard."

The client gives a command or triggers a scenario; the system picks an available group and executes.
