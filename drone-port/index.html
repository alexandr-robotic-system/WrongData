04 / MODULAR DRONE PORT

An autonomous station for one group of three.

The Modular Drone Port is the ground infrastructure a drone group returns to between missions.

04
  • Protected storageDrones are housed and shielded between missions.
  • Automatic roofOpens before launch, closes on recovery.
  • Autonomous chargingNo manual handling between flights.
  • Precision landingRTK/GNSS, AprilTag markers and V-shaped guides.
  • WeatherproofingProtection from rain and the elements.
  • Comms & computeOnboard coordination for every mission.
  • Fleet statusLive readiness for the next launch.
  • Reporting dataFlights, events and surveyed zones, logged.

One port serves one group of three drones. Scaling is straightforward: more area or more tasks call for more groups, and more ports joined into a single platform.
